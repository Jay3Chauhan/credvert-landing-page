    Help Center

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does Credvert pick the best card for my purchase?
    Credvert maintains a real-time catalog of reward multiplier rules including SmartBuy portals (5X–10X), category exclusions (fuel, rent), monthly spend caps, and point redemption values. When you enter a spend, the algorithm calculates precise net financial return — including 18% GST deduction on foreign exchange markup if you're paying abroad.
    Is granting SMS permission safe?
    Yes — Credvert runs an isolated Dart regex parser directly inside your phone's RAM. Full 16-digit card numbers, OTPs, and raw SMS text bodies are strictly discarded by design. Only anonymized spend metadata (amount, merchant, card suffix) is used to compute analytics. No SMS content ever reaches any server.
    How does foreign exchange markup calculation work?
    Most Indian banks charge between 2.0% and 3.5% forex markup, plus 18% GST on that fee (e.g., 3.5% × 1.18 = 4.13% effective total). Credvert deducts this exact amount from your reward rate, so you see true net return. For a card like ICICI Emeralde (1.5% markup = 1.77% effective), international spending is actually profitable over standard cards.
    What about iOS? Apple doesn't allow SMS reading.
    On iOS, Credvert provides a one-tap clipboard paste parser. Copy a bank alert and tap "Parse Clipboard" — the parsing still happens 100% on your device with the same zero-knowledge guarantees. You can also log spends manually with the same category intelligence.
    Can I delete my account and all data permanently?
    Yes, unconditionally. Under the DPDP Act 2023, GDPR and CCPA you have an absolute Right to Erasure. Go to Account → Delete Account in-app, or use our online deletion portal. All records, cards, and anonymized hashes are permanently purged within 24 hours.
